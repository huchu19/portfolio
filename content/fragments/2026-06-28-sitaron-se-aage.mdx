<span lang="ur" dir="rtl" className="urdu">ستاروں سے آگے جہاں اور بھی ہیں</span>

"Beyond the stars there are worlds yet more." — Iqbal. He wrote this for a generation told their sky had a ceiling. I read it every time a rejection email arrives.
