Bamboo is the fastest-growing organism on earth, and the name stuck because the product is about growth on both sides of a marketplace: inventors plant a pitch, investors grow a portfolio, and the platform's job is to keep the two honest with each other.

## The shape of it

Inventors upload a 60-second pitch video and their supporting documents — business plans, financials — and pay a listing fee ($49, or $199 with a verified badge). Investors browse the grove, watch pitches, and submit investments against real-time funding progress.

The repo is a monorepo with three residents: a `shared` package of TypeScript types and constants, a Next.js 14 web app, and an Expo React Native app. The shared types are the whole trick — web and mobile argue with the same compiler, so a schema change breaks loudly in both places instead of quietly in one.

## What building two clients taught me

Firebase carries auth, Firestore, and storage; Stripe is stubbed but shaped for real integration. The discipline that paid off was designing the data model once, in `shared`, before either client existed. Every time I was tempted to let the mobile app have "its own version" of a type, I was really being tempted to have two products.
