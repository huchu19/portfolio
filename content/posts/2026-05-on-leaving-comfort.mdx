*Draft — rewrite me, or keep me; I was written for the layout, not the ages.*

I closed the door the quiet way,\
the way you shut a book\
you mean to finish —\
thumb still in the page.

Comfort is a room\
that learns your shape too well;\
sit long enough\
and the chair begins to hold you back.

I packed the things that argue:\
two languages, one prayer,\
a map my father folded\
along different lines than mine.

Somewhere over the night\
the plane forgot my name,\
and I was only weather —\
moving, and moved.

If you ask what I left,\
I'll say: nothing. The page is kept.\
The book stays open in that house,\
and I am still the thumb.
